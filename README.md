# Sales-Analytics-Tracker
I designed and developed an interactive Sales Analytics Tracker in Power BI to help business leaders monitor sales person performance, uncover profit opportunities in products, regions and geographical locations, and make data-driven decisions at a glance.

<img width="587" height="335" alt="Sales Analytics Dashbaord" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/780d626d-47ac-4293-a113-afcb9f8df25c" />

**Project Objectives**

Track overall sales performance and profitability

Analyze product-level contribution to total profit

Identify top-performing regions and markets

Evaluate sales team performance and contribution

Monitor key KPIs such as profit margin, total customers, and cost efficiency

Enable dynamic filtering for product and geographic insights

**Methodology**

Data Cleaning: Processed raw sales data, handled inconsistencies, and ensured accuracy

Data Transformation: Developed calculated metrics including Profit, Profit %, and Profit Margin

Data Modeling: Built relationships between Product, Geography, and Sales Personnel

Dashboard Design: Created intuitive and visually appealing layouts for executive consumption

**Visualization Techniques:**

KPI cards for high-level metrics

Bar charts for product and regional comparisons

Donut chart for regional profit distribution

Tabular view for sales personnel performance

Interactivity: Implemented slicers for Product and Geography filtering

**Key Skills Demonstrated**

Data Analysis & Business Intelligence

KPI Development & Performance Tracking

Data Visualization & Dashboard Design

Insight Generation & Storytelling

Stakeholder-focused Reporting

Analytical Thinking & Problem Solving

**Business Impact**

Identified top-performing products driving the highest profit, enabling better inventory and marketing decisions

Revealed regional performance gaps, supporting targeted expansion strategies

Highlighted high-performing sales representatives, informing incentive and reward systems

Provided a clear view of profit margins (54%), helping assess overall business efficiency

Enabled leadership to quickly monitor business health through a single dashboard
